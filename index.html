Kayce Laine

Sound Bath Facilitator

Kayce is a full-time Certified Sound, Crystal, and Reiki Practitioner based in Nashville, with a music degree from the University of Virginia. She earned her Diploma in Integral Sound Healing with the Sound Healing Academy, where she also serves as an Associate Teacher. Her work has been trusted by organizations including TikTok, Soho House, Lululemon, and MusiCares, and she has headlined sound healing events at the Nashville Symphony.
